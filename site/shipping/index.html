Shipping and returns.

How your order gets to you, and what happens if something isn't right.

Shipping

Orders ship within the United States for a flat $8. By default I'll use USPS Ground or equivalent. If you need a piece more quickly, send me an email and I'll do my best.

In-stock, non-customized orders typically ship within a day or two. If I'm out of town it may take longer - I'll let you know via email if that's the case.

Local pickup

Pickup in Madison, Wisconsin is free. Choose "Pick up" in the cart and I'll email you to set a time.

Returns and refunds

If you're unhappy with a purchase for any reason, please let me know. I'll send you a refund as soon as I get the item back.

Commissions are covered on the commissions page.

Questions

Email ross@golemcraftworks.com and I'll sort it out with you.
